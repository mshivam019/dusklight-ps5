PlayStation button artwork reference

Source: https://github.com/griches/PS5_Dusklight
Revision: 577c76212bec33198d69ca727433573ec00beb78
Original generator: ps5/art/make-glyphs.py (MIT)
Original images: ps5/art/glyphs/*.png

The original five-symbol approach is extended here to complete neutral button
caps, inventory/dialogue shoulder icons, Options and stick prompts. The generator
retains Kenney's outlines and fills their interiors instead of stripping the rim.
Options and stick marks are simple independent geometric artwork in the generator.
The symbols and shoulder outlines use Kenney's Input Prompts, CC0:
https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts
The original CC0 input images and license are under ps5/art/kenney.
No image data from a game disc is included. Texture filenames identify the
original button resources; the PNG pixels are independent replacement artwork.

Default mapping: A=Cross, B=Circle, X=Square, Y=Triangle, L=L2, R=R2, Z=R1.
button-textures.json identifies 25 original resources (20 replacement prompts,
5 obsolete backing shapes). Each identifier was found on PAL GZ2P01. Matching
filenames do not establish visual correctness; manual console testing is required.
