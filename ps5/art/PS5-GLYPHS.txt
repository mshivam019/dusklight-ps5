PlayStation button artwork reference

Source: https://github.com/griches/PS5_Dusklight

Original generator: ps5/art/make-glyphs.py (MIT)
Original images: ps5/art/glyphs/*.png

The five replacement textures shipped under res/ps5-glyphs are unchanged from
that reference. They are generated from Kenney's Input Prompts, CC0:
https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts
The original CC0 input images and license are under ps5/art/kenney.
No image data from a game disc is included. Texture filenames identify the
original button resources; the PNG pixels are independent replacement artwork.

Default mapping: A=Cross, B=Circle, X=Square, Y=Triangle, Z=R1.
The reference labels these resource hashes as USA; appearance with the PAL
disc must be checked on the console before claiming PAL glyph support.
